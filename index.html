The Soft Hub Problem

In numerical simulations of non-perturbative quantum gravity (CDT), random triangulation manifolds undergo phase transitions into a pathological branched polymer phase where topological hubs act as unphysical wormholes, collapsing the spectral dimension.

⚠️ The Pathology

A small subset of vertices acquire anomalously large coordination numbers (d_u ≫ ⟨d⟩). When probed with unweighted random walks (q=0), these hubs broadcast walkers across the universe in a single step, causing the spectral dimension to collapse: D_s → 0.

The Hub Impedance Solution

Instead of censoring hubs (which violates diffeomorphism invariance), we introduce a weighted physical edge impedance that neutralizes their shortcut capability while preserving statistical ergodicity. 

# Hub impedance weight: suppresses topological shortcuts
def hub_impedance_weight(d_u, d_v, q=0.25):
    """Physical edge impedance for simplicial gravity."""
    return (max(1, d_u) * max(1, d_v)) ** (-q)
